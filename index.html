I'm the Chief Customer Officer at Swirl®, a startup building AI sales agents for high-consideration consumer brands — automotive, home improvement, consumer tech.

My work revolves around one question: as the interface shifts from scroll to ask, who owns the decision? I believe every enterprise brand will need its own AI sales layer — not a chatbot, but a revenue-generating engine that guides buyers with confidence.

I've launched AI-powered buyer experiences with BYD and Al-Futtaim across the UAE and KSA, represented Swirl at CES 2026 in the Eureka Park zone, and spent years studying what makes high-ticket buying feel effortless and trustworthy.

I write about the tectonic shifts happening at the edge of AI, commerce, and brand strategy — for founders and operators who want to think clearly about what's next.
